# Learning Repeated Market Game (LRMG)

## Behavioral Experimental Study of Inflation Dynamics and Anticipatory Learning

### Author
Malek Othman  
Professional Master's in Economic and Financial Engineering  
University of Carthage — 2024

---

## Overview

This research project investigates the persistence of inflation through the lens of behavioral economics, experimental economics, and game theory.

The study introduces a Learning Repeated Market Game (LRMG) framework designed to analyze how bounded rationality and anticipatory learning influence price convergence and market stability.

Unlike standard rational expectation models, the LRMG framework explores adaptive human behavior under imperfect information and repeated strategic interaction.

---

## Research Objectives

The project investigates:

- inflation persistence
- bounded rationality in market systems
- anticipatory learning mechanisms
- repeated market interactions
- strategic producer-consumer adaptation
- equilibrium formation under imperfect information

---

## Core Hypotheses

### H1
Rational agents operating with static expectations converge toward stable market equilibrium.

### H2
Agents with bounded rationality and anticipatory learning converge toward stable market equilibrium.

---

## Methodology

The project combines:

- behavioral economics
- repeated market games
- experimental economics
- simulation algorithms
- strategic interaction analysis

### Simulation Component
A repeated market game algorithm was developed to study price and quantity dynamics under static expectations.

### Experimental Component
A laboratory-style behavioral experiment was conducted using producer-consumer interactions under imperfect information constraints.

Participants interacted in repeated strategic market environments involving:
- pricing decisions
- budget constraints
- adaptive expectations
- anticipatory learning

---

## Main Findings

### Static Rationality Fails
Simulation results indicate that rational agents relying solely on static expectations fail to achieve stable price convergence.

The system instead exhibits:
- price divergence
- unstable oscillations
- self-reinforcing inflationary dynamics

### Bounded Rationality Improves Convergence
Experimental results suggest that bounded rationality combined with anticipatory learning improves market stabilization.

Observed behavioral patterns include:
- adaptive pricing
- strategic anticipation
- gradual convergence toward equilibrium
- differentiated learning speeds among agents
